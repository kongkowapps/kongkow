<details>
<summary>Notes on comparison accuracy</summary>

**Social platforms**
- **X tips** link to third-party services (Venmo, Cash App, Bitcoin) — X does not process the payment itself
- **X group chats** launched in late 2025 via X Chat with E2E encryption
- **Facebook tips** are via Stars ($0.01 each) on live streams and Reels; **subscriptions** ($0.99–$99.99/month) unlock exclusive content
- **Facebook paid groups** were tested as "paid subgroups" (2018–2021) but never broadly launched
- **Reddit tips** are via Gold awards; creators earn through the Contributor Program based on Gold received and karma
- **Reddit channels** are Discord-like Chat Channels within subreddits for real-time conversations
- **Reddit** discontinued Community Points (crypto tokens) in late 2023
- **Instagram tips** are via Gifts (Reels) and Badges (Lives); **subscriptions** give access to exclusive posts
- **TikTok tips** are via Gifts/Coins, primarily on LIVE; **subscriptions** are for LIVE content access
- **VK tips** are via VK Donut (recurring supporter donations); **subscriptions** give access to exclusive posts and donor-only chats
- **VK channels** — VK "Public Pages" function as broadcast-style content pages
- **VK** supports in-chat money transfers via bank cards (Russia only), not a stored wallet

**Messaging platforms**
- **Telegram tips** are via Star Reactions; **PPV** is via Paid Media (Stars); **paid access** via Star Subscriptions for channels
- **WhatsApp** has in-app payments (WhatsApp Pay) in India and Brazil only; **channel subscriptions** with paid exclusive updates are rolling out
- **Signal** removed its earlier MobileCoin payment experiment; it is now a pure messaging app with no payment features

**Kongkow**
- Uses per-transaction pricing (one-time payments), not recurring monthly subscriptions — a fundamentally different model
- All payments are native SOL on Solana, settled on-chain

</details>

Nine platforms compared. All of them pick a lane: social *or* messaging *or* payments. Kongkow does all three — with a native Solana wallet, not a bolt-on. And at 0.1%, the lowest creator fee of any platform.

## Features

### Wallet — the core

- **Send SOL by username** — no wallet addresses, no copy-paste
- **Tip creators instantly** — one tap, settled on Solana
- **Pay-per-view content** — charge for premium posts
- **Paid groups** — users pay to join, auto-unlock, no admin approval
- **Creator coins** — launch and trade social tokens on a bonding curve (via Bags)
- **Token swaps** — swap tokens in-app with 1% fee (via Bags)
- **Token Launchpad** — deploy new tokens instantly
- **Earnings dashboard** — track tips, sales, and referral rewards
- **Export or recover** — export your private key anytime

### Social

- Public and private posts with likes, comments, reshares, and saves
- Stories with view tracking
- Follow feeds — see posts from people you follow
- Scheduled posts — write now, publish later
- AI-enhanced writing — improve your posts before publishing
- User search and profile discovery

### Messaging

- Direct messages with media, files, and reply threads
- Group chats with channels (text and announcement), categories, and roles
- Send money inside any conversation
- Soft delete, mute, and read receipts
- **Mini Apps** — interactive widgets inside chat

- **Mini Apps** — interactive widgets inside chat (e.g. specialized wallets, games)

### Bot Platform

- **Standalone Bots** — centralized AI, decentralized execution
- **Kongkow SDK** — unified library for building financial bots
- **Born with a Wallet** — every bot gets a Solana wallet automatically
- **Withdrawals** — bots can send earnings to their owner securely

### User Growth

- **Referrals** — earn 0.0005 SOL when invited friends activate (first tip/fund)
- **Referral Cap** — max 50 rewards per 30 days to prevent abuse

## Security

Your wallet. Your keys. Our job to protect them.

- **Encrypted wallet keys** — AES-256-GCM, keys never leave secure storage
- **Row-Level Security** — enforced on every database table
- **Strict CSP** — no unsafe-eval, no inline script injection
- **Rate limiting** — on all sensitive endpoints
- **Input validation** — every API route, every request
- **HTTPS with HSTS** — preloaded, always encrypted in transit
